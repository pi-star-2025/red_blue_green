## In Between World - Checkerboard Time

The unconscious time only sees 1s and 0s, which are genetic decisions, via the eyes. This is still vision, but smaller than conscious vision, which is more complex than 1s/0s.

This is possible by seeing with 99+ sides. 1/99 ≈ .01010101010101010101

## Dying Awake

There is the legend that you can die awake by becoming this in-between-world. 

You essentially become your genetics that are in-between the cells and fibers of your body, and also are mapped to the color you see in front of you.

You could still exist in “lower reality”, and your body would operate based on the music of the genetics, which is enough to surrogate for consciousness.

Then above, you could exist with a speedy, cartoon consciousness which sees the future/past as a map to reality. Seeing the future is the top reason to exist up there.
